Two visual languages behind the practice: Recognition Frameworks (how a person comes to see themselves) and Treatment Models (how the work is structured). Six interactive models across the three areas — individual, relationship, and sex.

Part One

Recognition Frameworks

Interactive light-fields. Each enacts the way a person begins to recognize what's inside — one gesture per area: listen, adjust, reveal.

01

Individual

— returning to yourself / inner parts

At the center is you. Around you move several parts — the watchful one, the critic, the tired one, the one that still hopes. They begin scattered and unsettled. Focus on each to hear it; then let them settle into orbit around you.

02

Relationship

— two independents, and the third thing between them

Two people, each whole. Bring them together and something new appears between you — a shared "us." But closeness isn't a matter of more. Too far apart and there's nothing between you; too merged and you lose yourselves. Health lives in the balance.

03

Sex

— shedding shame / re-meeting desire

The things hardest to say sit here, dimmed and scattered. Some are truths kept quiet by shame; others are rules we were handed and never questioned. Touch each to bring it into the light — the truths can be met, the rules can be seen for what they are. Together they become a warm, connected whole.

Treatment Model 03

Sex Therapy Radial

A small universe with four connected dimensions — mind, body, others, and self. Explore any region; each belongs to the whole.

Mind
Thoughts, beliefs, fantasies, anxiety, stories.
Others
Partners, attraction, boundaries, communication.
Body
Sensation, pleasure, arousal, pain, embodiment.
Self
Identity, values, agency, culture, self-acceptance.
All of these parts shape your experience. We explore them together.

Sexual wellbeing includes mind, body, others, and self. We explore all of it, openly, gently, and at your pace.
